Shorts Studio

A personal publishing tool run by Doğan Koç.

Shorts Studio turns news from official sources into short vertical videos and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channels:

  • Byte Frame Visuals: AI and technology news
  • SportFrameVisuals: sports news

How it uses Google sign-in

The owner signs in with Google to let the tool upload finished videos to these two channels and read their view statistics (views, likes, comments, average percentage viewed, subscribers gained and views by country). Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is uploaded.

The tool is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.
